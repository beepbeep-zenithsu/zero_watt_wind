# Task 01: Production component specification

## Draft

A production assembly needs a weatherproof rotor/hub, load-rated shafts, two bevel stages, thrust/radial supports, guards, overspeed brake, mounting and corrosion protection. A adds an axial fan, sealed shaft sleeve, plenum, duct, cowl, screened intake and waterproof roof interface. B adds paddles, float frame, compartmented HDPE pontoons, mooring, guards and retrieval provisions. Two-site combined installation duplicates the core. PLA is retained for patterns and noncritical parts, not assumed safe as rooftop gears or permanent pontoons.
