The project repeats a claim linking 500 [S] ppm additional CO2 with a 15 [S]% cognitive-performance
reduction. The institutional description and primary-study record concern specic observational test
endpoints. A general causal productivity loss at this exact percentage was NOT VERIFIED and is

FAO describes oxygen depletion and reduced growth in Bangladesh ponds, including warm calm
nights and cloudy monsoon conditions. A Bangladesh heatwave report corroborates the mechanism
but does not quantify national losses attributable only to low DO. These sources do not establish
this prototype prevents sh kills. [18, 30]



1.1 A site-level loss exposure, not an avoided-loss promise
An illustrative 1,000 [A] kg stock at 200 [A] BDT/kg and a 10% [A] event loss exposes 20,000 [D]
BDT. Low/high loss fractions are 2% [A] / 30% [A]. The event probability and avoidable fraction
are NOT FOUND; no expected-value benet is booked. Time spent manually splashing, fetching
fuel or servicing the unit is likewise NOT FOUND; travel/labour allowances are in OPEX.

Using the illustrative room duty, clinic electricity costs 2,180 [D] BDT/year before xed fees and
taxes. This modest energy bill constrains an energy-only product price. Load-shedding coincidence
with useful wind is NOT FOUND. Neither a mortality-reduction total nor a sh-yield uplift is
fabricated.

13 Environmental screening and end of life
The mechanical core uses no operating electricity, but it has embodied materials, fabrication, lo-
gistics, lubricant and replacement impacts. A transparent screening calculation is preferable to a
zero-carbon claim.    The DoE published factor is 0.62 [S] kg CO2/kWh for the stated older scal
periods, published in its January notice. This historical average factor is not a prediction of future
marginal displacement or a veried carbon-credit methodology. [28]

Gross avoided emissions equal displaced grid kWh times the grid factor.          Net screening benet
subtracts assumed embodied emissions. Foreground masses are 15 [A] kg steel, 4 [A] kg aluminium
and 3 [A] kg polymer for A, or 8 [A] kg for B. Assumed factors are 2.0 [A], 8.0 [A] and 3.0 [A]
kgCO2e/kg respectively.       These are screening placeholders, not supplier EPDs; bounds span half
