# Task 03: Sustainable build methods

## Draft

Use a printed pattern and locally formed sheet-metal rotor, machined steel shafts and purchased sealed bearings. Recycled aluminium needs alloy traceability and fatigue inspection. PETG/ASA parts need UV and creep tests; recycled filament needs moisture control, coupon tests and batch traceability. Bamboo can be a replaceable noncritical support or jig only after load/rot treatment checks. Use recoverable HDPE floats, mechanical fasteners, replaceable wear parts and a take-back agreement.
