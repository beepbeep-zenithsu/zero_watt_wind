L50: # 3D Printer Filament Price in Bangladesh: PLA, PETG, ABS or TPU?

L67: ## PLA and PLA+: where almost everyone should start
L68: 
L69: PLA prints at a low temperature (roughly 190–220°C nozzle, 50–60°C bed), does not need an enclosure, barely warps and has almost no smell. If you have just bought your first printer, buy PLA or PLA+ and nothing else until your prints come out clean.
L70: PLA+ is PLA with additives that make it less brittle. A PLA+ part bends a little before it snaps, where plain PLA cracks. For brackets, holders, toys and anything that gets handled, PLA+ is the better default, and it costs about the same.
L71: 
L72: The one weakness that matters in Bangladesh: heat. PLA starts to soften at around 55–60°C. A part left on a car dashboard, on a rooftop or next to a hot motor will sag. For anything that sits in the sun or near heat, move up to PETG or ASA.
