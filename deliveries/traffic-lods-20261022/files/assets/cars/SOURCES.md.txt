rejected sources. `verified` refers to the listing/license review, not completed
acquisition, acceptable visual quality, or manufacturer endorsement. No game-ripped

license and modification notice in each GLB. Installation also generates credits

and attribution with redistributed files. CC licenses apply to the model author's
