# City material and lighting sources

Downloaded and verified on 2026-10-22. All maps are stored locally; the running
website does not call Poly Haven. Poly Haven publishes these assets under CC0:
https://polyhaven.com/license . Attribution is appreciated, not mandatory.

Metadata used to resolve exact download files:
https://api.polyhaven.com/files/kloppenheim_06_puresky ,
https://api.polyhaven.com/files/beige_wall_001 ,
https://api.polyhaven.com/files/brick_wall_001 .

The local HDRLoader is the Three.js r180 loader with its module import redirected
to the vendored Three.js module. Source:
https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/jsm/loaders/HDRLoader.js .
Three.js MIT license is retained in vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt.

The skyline GLBs are original Blender-authored geometry, generated by
scripts/blender/build_city_architecture.py. They are not downloaded models.

No third-party vehicle source or vehicle license has been verified in this
checkpoint. The eight catalog entries remain acquisition configurations;
missing car models continue to use the existing moving fallback fleet.
