# City traffic, riders and corner workstation

The website now has 18 moving cars and ten riders on eight circuits across the
connected city roads. Signals and shared junction reservations coordinate both
groups. Dry-road car cruise is 7.2 m/s (about 26 km/h); curves, queues, rain and
storms reduce speed. Movement retains 120 Hz steps, bounded jerk and eased heading.
Cyclist wheels, pedals and leg motion follow distance travelled, including stops.

All moving cars use genuine installed Jaguar E-Type, Porsche Taycan or De Tomaso
P72 models. Three of the requested eight types are available; the other five are
explicitly pending. Source and license details: [SOURCES.md](../../assets/cars/SOURCES.md).

The computer desk is in a rear corner, with one assigned operator facing the
screen when occupancy is nonzero. Other seats avoid that reserved area. Raising
occupancy only reveals additional predetermined seats, preserving the first
operator and existing seat positions. The layout supports 30 occupants in the
smallest room. Front-corner plants stay clear of the desk and seating.

These are headless Blender geometry/PBR reviews, not browser screenshots.
`mobility-validation.json` records actual loaded car IDs, replacement count,
routes, riders and seating. `installed-asset-validation.json` records checks on
the nine real vehicle LODs. Browser FPS and GPU metrics remain unmeasured.

Relevant complete source files:

- `src/graphics/road-network.js`: circuits and staggered initial positions.
- `src/graphics/traffic.js`: queues, shared reservations and smooth integration.
- `src/graphics/cyclists.js`: rider traffic and two-link leg/pedal motion.
- `src/graphics/occupant-layout.js`: reserved desk space and stable seat order.
- `src/cars/traffic-fleet.js`: actual installed models, LOD reuse, wheel and night-lamp motion.
- `scripts/blender/build_city_cyclists.py`: editable original bicycle/rider source.

Rebuild the rider with `node tools/run-blender.cjs scripts/blender/build_city_cyclists.py`.
For car rebuilds, see [TRAFFIC-PIPELINE.md](TRAFFIC-PIPELINE.md). Run `npm run build`
after source/registry changes; `npm start` serves the project on port 5500.
